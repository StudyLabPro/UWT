## Abstract for arXiv metadata

Unified Whole Theory (UWT) is proposed as a formal relational framework in which
space, time, motion, distance, mass, and energy are not primitive notions but
derived structures. The construction starts from a mereological whole, a lattice
of parts, distinguishability, and relations valued in a normalized ordered group.
Relational distance is obtained from normalized relation values, the triangle
inequality is derived from composition rather than postulated, time is introduced
as a structure of changes between states, and elementary mechanical quantities are
defined as invariants of relational change. The paper also presents an explicit
finite model establishing relative consistency of the basic axioms and lists
testable directions where the relational framework may differ from conventional
space-time-first approaches. The submission is bilingual: English first, Russian
second.
